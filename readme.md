Hello! I am a linguist, <strong>English teacher, translator, and writer</strong>, with over <strong>7 years of experience</strong> transforming how students and professionals connect with fluency.

My pedagogical approach moves away from traditional mechanical memorization and focuses on how the human brain processes, stores, and retrieves a second language naturally and deeply. Through strategies designed to reduce cognitive load and respect the pillars of memory retention, I guide my students toward authentic, confident communication tailored to their daily routines.

- 🌍 Focus on high-performance communication and linguistic literacy.
- 📖 Author of the digital book <strong>"A mente que fala inglês"</strong>, centered on an active learning mindset.
- 💡 Advocate for personalized, elegant, and intellectually stimulating education.
